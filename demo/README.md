# Taraneh-ASR

Hosting this Gradio app on Hugging Face needs a PRO subscription. The app itself is ready to upload.

درود بر پارسی‌زبانان،  
و بر آن خاکِ گرامی.

Upload or record up to 30 seconds of Persian speech. Pick one of the five models. Taraneh-WMedium, Taraneh-WLarge, Taraneh-Q0.6B, and Taraneh-Q1.7B can also return word times.

This Space runs on a free CPU, so the larger models are slow. The first run of a model also downloads its weights.

Models: [Taraneh collection](https://huggingface.co/collections/areffarhadi/taraneh-asr-persian-automatic-speech-recognition)

Paper and code: [github.com/areffarhadi/persian-asr](https://github.com/areffarhadi/persian-asr)
